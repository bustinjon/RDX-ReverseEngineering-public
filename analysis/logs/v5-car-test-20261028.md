## Key Observations

### 1. Honda wins the USB race instantly (CONFIRMED)
Honda's GalReceiver fires on USB_DEVICE_ATTACHED within the same broadcast cycle.
Our service also receives it, but Honda has MANAGE_USB — no permission dialog needed.
Honda does the AOAP switch, phone re-enumerates, Honda starts ProjectionActivity.

### 2. Our service detects AOAP correctly (CONFIRMED)
437ms after AOAP re-enumeration (561553→561990), our service spots it.
Waits ~1s for stability, then requests permission. This all works.

### 3. Permission is ALWAYS denied (CRITICAL BLOCKER)
11 consecutive attempts, all `granted=false`. The overlay assist shows and guides taps,
but permission is never granted. Honda's ProjectionActivity is fullscreen in front.

### 4. Resource resolution warning (SUSPICIOUS)
`PackageManager: Failure retrieving resources for com.rdx.fullscreen.usbhelper: Resource ID #0x0`
appears on every permission attempt. This may indicate the system can't resolve our
device_filter.xml, which would prevent automatic USB permission matching.

### 5. Honda's AOAP switch timing
- Phone plugs in at 560789
- Honda detects at 560791 (2ms later)
- Phone detaches at 560905 (116ms for AOAP switch)
- Phone re-enumerates at 561553 (648ms after detach = 764ms total)
- Honda's mSwitchingTimer was set to 3000ms — phone came back in 680ms

### 6. Non-AOAP path was not attempted
Service was started without `-a usb.HONDA_STOPPED` and `forceStopSucceeded` was false,
so it correctly did NOT attempt AOAP switch on the non-AOAP OnePlus device.
It only tried to claim the device after Honda's AOAP switch.

## Root Cause Analysis

The permission denial is the same issue from previous tests. Possible causes:
1. Honda's MANAGE_USB gives it exclusive device access — system refuses second grant
2. Honda's fullscreen ProjectionActivity blocks/intercepts the permission dialog
3. The "Resource ID #0x0" error means our USB filter isn't matched → no auto-grant
4. The system's UsbPermissionActivity is behind Honda's window and auto-cancels
